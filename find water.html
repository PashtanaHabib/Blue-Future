Discover Earth's Water

Water shapes the planet in ways we often overlook. From vast oceans and winding rivers to hidden groundwater and frozen glaciers, Earth's water is constantly moving, changing, and connecting every part of the planet.

A project raising awareness about the global water crisis, mapping water sources, and giving citizens a simple way to report and help solve water problems.
