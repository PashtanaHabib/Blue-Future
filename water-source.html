Fresh Water Sources

Fresh water comes from several natural sources. These sources provide the water people, animals, plants and communities need to survive.

🌊

Rivers

Rivers carry fresh water across the land and are important sources for communities, agriculture and ecosystems.

🏞️

Lakes

Lakes store large amounts of fresh water and support people, wildlife and plants.

💧

Groundwater

Groundwater is stored below the Earth's surface and is accessed through wells.

🌧️

Rainwater

Rain provides fresh water naturally and can be collected and stored for later use.

Did You Know?

Only a small percentage of the water on Earth is fresh water that people can use.

Most of Earth's water is found in oceans, while fresh water is stored in glaciers, groundwater, lakes and rivers.

Protecting water sources today helps ensure that future generations have access to clean and safe water.
