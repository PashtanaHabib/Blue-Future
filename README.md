# 🌊 Blue Future

**Blue Future** is a modern educational and awareness website focused on the global water crisis, water scarcity, and the importance of protecting and managing our water resources.

This project was created as a **group practice project** to apply and improve our front-end development skills using **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap**.

## 📌 About The Project

Water is one of the most important resources for life, yet many communities around the world are facing serious challenges related to water scarcity, pollution, and limited access to clean water.

The goal of **Blue Future** is to create an attractive and informative platform that raises awareness about these problems and encourages people to use water responsibly.
